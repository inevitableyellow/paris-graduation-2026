Parking & transport
Free parking is available in the Blue Lot surrounding Crisler Center (lots SC4, SC5, SC6, SC7), with entrances on Stadium Boulevard and East Keech Avenue / Greene Street.

ADA parking is available outside the Northeast entrance on a first-come, first-served basis — a state-issued disability placard is required.

Note: University parking permits are not valid for this event. Please leave bags in your vehicle as Crisler Center has a strict no-bag policy.

View parking map (PDF) →
Dress code
Smart casual is a safe bet, but honestly, wear whatever makes you feel your best! It's a celebration! 🎉 Keep in mind that Crisler Center can vary in temperature depending on where you're sitting, so layers are your friend. And since it's April in Ann Arbor, a light jacket for outdoors is always a good idea.
Note on tickets
All graduates and guests require a ticket. Children over 2 years old require their own ticket. Each ticket QR code is unique and single-use.
Prohibited items at Crisler Center
The following are strictly prohibited inside all U-M commencement venues:
  • All bags (including backpacks and purses)
  • Aerosol and spray cans
  • Containers of any kind (coolers, thermoses, cups, bottles, cans, flasks)
  • Animals (except service animals)
  • Signs and flags with or without flagpoles
  • Food of any kind
  • Alcoholic beverages
  • Noisemakers (whistles, air horns, vuvuzelas, etc.)
  • Strollers
  • Video cameras, tripods, selfie sticks, cameras with lens longer than 6 inches
  • Weapons
  • Cigarettes, vapes, e-cigarettes, and all smoking devices
